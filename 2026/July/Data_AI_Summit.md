## 📝 Reflection from Marina of Minds – Data & AI Summit
![Marina of Minds – Data & AI Summit](https://github.com/cooljay17/Learning_Journal/blob/main/Images/LTM.png)
### ✅ Three Things I Learned
- **FAIR Data Principles** are essential for making data Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable, enabling trustworthy AI.
- AI is evolving from **Systems of Insights** to **Systems of Actions**, bringing intelligence closer to where the data resides.
- Successful AI adoption requires more than technology—it needs **business acumen, storytelling, pattern discovery, data governance, and high-quality data**.

### 💡 One Idea That Challenged My Thinking
I always believed that building a powerful AI model was the key to success. The sessions made me realize that **without quality data and business context, even the most advanced AI can produce misleading results.**

### 🔍 One Concept I Want to Explore Further
**FAIR Data Principles**—I want to learn how implementing FAIR principles can improve data quality, governance, and AI readiness in enterprise data platforms.

### 🏦 One Way It Relates to My Core Banking Project
The summit reinforced the importance of **data quality and governance** in my Oracle Core Banking project. Maintaining accurate transaction data, validations, audit logs, and standardized data structures will make the system more reliable today and AI-ready for future fraud detection, customer insights, and intelligent banking applications.
